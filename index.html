Built on the open social web

Keep everything that works today. Gain what you never had.

Rostrum runs on the AT Protocol, the open network behind Bluesky and a growing family of apps known as the Atmosphere. You keep the reach, speed and familiarity of the big platforms. Ownership, verification and choice come with it.

What you keep

The public square you already know

  • Real-time public conversationPosts, threads, replies, quotes, reposts, video and direct messages. It works the way you expect.
  • An audience that is already thereMillions of people, and many of Europe's journalists, already use Bluesky and other Atmosphere apps. Your Rostrum account reaches all of them from day one.
  • Familiar apps on every phoneRostrum is your account, not another app to learn. Post from Bluesky, mu or any app on the network.
  • No need to leave anywhere overnightPublish once and cross-post to the networks you still use. An open bridge also carries your posts to Mastodon and the wider fediverse.
  • Discovery, embeds and press pick-upSearch, trending topics and shareable posts that embed in websites, newsletters and news coverage.
  • Your existing followersAlready on Bluesky? Move your account to Rostrum with every follower intact. Coming from elsewhere? Find the people you already follow.

What you gain

An account that answers to you

  • Your audience is yoursYour followers belong to your account, not to a company. Move to another provider at any time and they come with you.
  • No owner between you and votersNo single company decides who sees you. People choose their own feeds, so no owner can throttle, sell or switch off your voice.
  • Verification that means somethingChecked against the Parliament's own register. It can't be bought or faked.
  • Moderation you choosePick the moderation services that protect your replies, and change them whenever you like.
  • European jurisdictionHosted in Germany by a Dutch non-profit, under the GDPR and the Digital Services Act.
  • A complete, portable recordEvery post, poll and answer is yours to export and archive. It stays with you after your term ends.

How verification works

Three steps from reservation to verified badge

  1. Reserve your handle

    Tell us your name and member state. We hold firstname-lastname.rostrum.eu for you until the founding cohort opens.

  2. Confirm it's you

    We match you against the European Parliament's public list of Members and confirm through your official Parliament email address.

  3. Go live, verified

    Your badge is issued and recognised network-wide. Bring your Bluesky followers across, invite your team and start posting.

Questions

What Members and their offices ask

Will I lose reach compared with the platforms I use now?

No. Your Rostrum account works in Bluesky and every other Atmosphere app from day one, where millions of people and many journalists are already active. You can cross-post to the networks you still use, and an open bridge carries your posts to Mastodon and the fediverse.

Do I have to leave the platforms I use now?

No. Most Members will run Rostrum alongside their existing accounts at first. Rostrum becomes the place you own, verified and portable, and the others become channels you publish to.

Is Rostrum an official EU service?

No. Rostrum is proposed as an independent, not-for-profit service. It is not run, funded or endorsed by the European Parliament or any EU institution. It verifies Members using the Parliament's public register.

Is it really free?

Yes, for every Member and every political group. Rostrum is designed as public-interest infrastructure funded by foundations and grants. There are no ads, no paid reach and no data sales.

I already have a Bluesky account. What happens to it?

You can move it to Rostrum and keep your followers, posts and history. Your current handle can redirect to your new verified one, so nobody loses track of you.

Can I leave Rostrum and take my followers with me?

Yes. That's the point of the open social web: your account can move to any compatible provider, and your followers move with it.

Can my staff post for me?

Yes, with the permissions you set. Every draft, approval and post records who did it, so accountability stays with the Member.

How are citizens verified for polls and petitions?

Taking part is open to everyone. Citizens who want their response counted as verified can prove residence in a member state with the EU Digital Identity Wallet, without sharing their name. Verified and open results are always shown separately.

What about national, regional and local representatives?

Rostrum starts with Members of the European Parliament because the public register makes verification reliable. National, regional and local representatives follow in later phases.
